Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our hotel, rooms, and services.

Booking & Reservations

How do I make a reservation? ›

You can book online through our booking page, call our reservations team, or visit us in person. We also accept bookings through major travel platforms and booking agencies.

What is your cancellation policy? ›

Cancellations made 7 days or more before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations within 7 days are subject to one night's charge. Late cancellations (within 24 hours) are non-refundable.

What are the check-in and check-out times? ›

Standard check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged based on availability. Please contact the front desk to arrange.

Do you offer group discounts? ›

Yes, we offer special rates for groups of 10 or more rooms. Contact our events team for customized group packages and pricing.

Is there a deposit required for reservations? ›

A deposit equal to one night's stay is required to confirm reservations. The deposit is credited toward your final bill.

Rooms & Facilities

What amenities are included in the rooms? ›

All rooms feature air conditioning, private bathroom, flat-screen TV, work desk, complimentary Wi-Fi, and premium bedding. Deluxe and executive rooms include additional amenities.

Do your rooms have Wi-Fi? ›

Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available in all rooms and throughout the hotel.

Can I request a specific room type? ›

Yes, you can request your preferred room type during booking. We'll do our best to accommodate based on availability.

Do you have accessible rooms for guests with disabilities? ›

Yes, we have specially equipped accessible rooms with features to assist guests with mobility challenges. Please mention any special requirements during booking.

Is there parking available? ›

Yes, we offer complimentary parking for all hotel guests in our secure parking area.

Dining & Services

What dining options are available? ›

Our restaurant serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner with authentic Ethiopian and international cuisine. Room service is available 24/7.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions? ›

Absolutely. We can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and other dietary requirements. Please inform us when making a reservation.

What business services are available? ›

We offer business center facilities, meeting rooms, conference halls, AV equipment, catering services, and high-speed internet for business travelers.

Can you host private events? ›

Yes, we host weddings, conferences, corporate events, and celebrations. Contact our events team for customized packages and planning assistance.

Payment & Special Offers

What payment methods do you accept? ›

We accept cash, credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfers, and digital payment methods. Please inquire for specific options.

Are there current special offers? ›

Yes, we have various promotions including early booking discounts, weekend getaway packages, corporate rates, and long-stay savings. Visit our offers page for current deals.

Is there a loyalty program? ›

We offer special rates for repeat guests and loyalty incentives. Contact us for details on joining our frequent guest program.

Do you have a best price guarantee? ›

Yes, if you find a lower rate elsewhere for the same room and dates, we'll match it and offer an additional discount.

Still Have Questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Contact our team directly.
