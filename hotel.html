Our Journey

Hatsey Kaleb Hotel is a family-owned establishment founded to create a welcoming space for travelers seeking comfort, culture, and outstanding hospitality in Tigray.

The Story Behind Hatsey Kaleb Hotel

Hatsey Kaleb Hotel was created for travelers who want more than a room — they want thoughtful hospitality, genuine local warmth, and the ease of a well-run stay in the heart of Tigray.

From the first welcome at reception to the final cup of coffee, every detail is shaped around comfort, discretion, and memorable service. We host business travelers, families, event guests, and explorers who value a calm, well-looked-after place to land.

With 20 rooms, a welcoming restaurant, and spaces designed for meetings and celebrations, we blend local character with practical luxury — making every stay feel personal, productive, and easy.

FAQ

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is available from 2:00 PM and check-out is until 11:00 AM. Early arrival or late departure can be arranged when available.

Is breakfast included in my stay?

Breakfast is included in selected room packages. Please check your booking details or contact reception for full meal options.

Do you offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel and event areas.

Can I book an event or meeting room?

Yes, our meeting and event spaces are available for conferences, weddings, and business gatherings. Contact us for availability and pricing.

Do you offer airport shuttle services?

Shuttle services are available on request. Please contact our front desk in advance to arrange airport transfers.
