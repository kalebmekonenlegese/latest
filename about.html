About Hatsey Kaleb Hotel

A refined heritage of hospitality, comfort, and cultural pride in the heart of Tigray.

Our story, reimagined for modern travelers

Hatsey Kaleb Hotel combines timeless Ethiopian warmth with polished service, becoming a trusted base for leisure travelers, business guests, and celebration-minded visitors.

The Hatsey Kaleb legacy

Founded as a family-led hospitality house, Hatsey Kaleb has grown into a destination defined by exceptional rooms, thoughtful events, and an atmosphere that feels personal from arrival to departure.

Every experience here is shaped by attention to detail, local pride, and authentic service that helps guests feel at home.

Mission, vision and values

We are committed to creating memorable stays through comfort, professionalism, cultural celebration, and responsible hospitality.

  • Exceptional comfort in every room and suite
  • Thoughtful service that feels warm and attentive
  • Local culture embedded in every detail
  • Responsible operations that support the community

Leadership team

Our leadership combines hospitality expertise with a deep respect for the local community and the expectations of discerning travelers.

Hospitality staff

From reception to restaurant and events, our team is known for graceful service, quick support, and genuine care.

Community commitment
